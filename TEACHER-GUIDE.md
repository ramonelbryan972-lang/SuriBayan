# SuriBayan — Quick parent and teacher guide (Grades 5–6)

## Purpose
Teach upper-elementary students how Philippine government works and how to question public issues responsibly, without promoting political parties or assuming guilt.

## Suggested 25-minute classroom activity

1. **5 minutes:** Ask “Saan nanggagaling ang pondo ng public school?” Read the *Buwis* and *COA* lessons.
2. **5 minutes:** Read the **fictional classroom construction story**. Ask: “What documents would we need before deciding what happened?”
3. **7 minutes:** Use the **₱100 community budget simulation** in small groups. Have each group explain its priorities and tradeoffs.
4. **5 minutes:** Complete a **SURI** fact-check practice round, showing the difference between opinion, allegation, and a sourced court ruling.
5. **3 minutes:** Choose one question on the civic quiz and discuss why the wrong answers are wrong.

## Ground rules

- No student should be asked to endorse or oppose a politician, candidate, or party.
- Differentiate *allegations*, *audit findings*, *formal cases*, and *final rulings*.
- Ask about **systems and evidence**, not whether classmates' families hold the “right” political views.
- Children should not contact officials alone, visit unsafe sites, or publish personal information.
- Review any current cases before classroom use. The case summaries are reviewed as of **November 19, 2026**, not automatically updated.

## Sample reflection prompts

- Bakit hindi sapat ang headline lamang?
- Paano magagamit nang tapat at patas ang pera ng bayan?
- Kailan kailangang magtanong, at kailan kailangang maghintay sa mas kumpletong ebidensya?
- Ano ang pagkakaiba ng “iniimbestigahan” at “napatunayang guilty”?

## Note

This is an informal civic-literacy project and is not an official DepEd module or approved curriculum. Teachers should adapt it to local learner needs and classroom policies.
