# Contributing to SuriBayan

Salamat! Contributions are welcome, especially from Filipino educators, students, civic organizations, accessibility reviewers, and developers.

1. Open an issue describing the proposed improvement or factual correction.
2. Cite a **primary source** with a direct URL and publication date for every factual political or legal claim.
3. Clearly distinguish allegations, audit observations, formal charges, and final court rulings. Never imply guilt without evidence or a legally sound basis.
4. Use simple Filipino/Taglish that upper-elementary students can understand. Avoid sensational headlines, candidate endorsements, and partisan appeals aimed at children.
5. Label invented scenarios as fictional. Do not include personal details about children or private individuals.
6. Test on mobile and desktop and verify keyboard accessibility. Run `node --check app.js` before submitting a pull request.
7. Open a pull request and explain your sources, changes, and tests.

## Code of conduct

Discuss evidence and public issues respectfully. No doxxing, harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
